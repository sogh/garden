The aesthetic and discipline I'm trying to borrow:

- **Atomicity.** One idea per note. If it's growing two ideas, fork it.
- **Concept-oriented titles.** "Atomic notes resist drift" not "May 14 thoughts."
- **Aggressive linking.** Connections are the substrate. A note with no
  backlinks is suspicious.
- **Visible growth states.** Roughness is a feature, not embarrassment.
- **No publish boundary.** Writing IS the published thing.

What I'm NOT copying:

- The stacking-panes UI. Beautiful, but for someone reading my own notes back
  to me, a single column is fine.
- The exhaustive "evergreen notes should be..." discipline. I want to be able
  to write a 200-word "here's what I tried today" note without theorizing.
