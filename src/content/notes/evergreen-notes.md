An *evergreen note* is one that has matured past the half-formed stage. It
states a single durable idea, has been edited more than once, and is linked to
from at least a few other notes in the garden. Most seedlings never become
evergreens — and that's fine. The garden is allowed to have undergrowth.

The point is not to convert every seedling. It's to let the worthwhile ones
get tended without forcing the rest to disappear.
