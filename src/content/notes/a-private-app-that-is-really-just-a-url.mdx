`glance-png-server` is a PNG rendering server that acts as a Private App for
the [Glance Scroll](https://glance-led.dev/) — a chained LED panel that polls a
URL you give it and draws whatever PNG comes back. That's the entire integration
surface. No SDK, no app to install, no account. A URL, a PNG, and about four
seconds before it gives up.

Which means "writing an app" for it collapses into "rendering an image on a
timer", and everything interesting moves to what you choose to draw.

Each panel is 192×32 — three 64px modules chained into one continuous image —
and lands on the wire at 150–600 bytes. The constraints are unusually strict
(PNG only, 1 MB cap, and [APNG doesn't
animate](https://glance-led.dev/docs/private-apps/), which was found by testing
the hardware rather than by reading anything).

The sample data gives the farm away, though: seed order arrives, hive
inspection, grange meeting.

Code is at [sogh/glance-png-server](https://github.com/sogh/glance-png-server);
the device's own developer docs for private apps are
[here](https://glance-led.dev/docs/private-apps/), and `docs/DEVICE.md` in the
repo is the version with the undocumented behaviour filled in.
