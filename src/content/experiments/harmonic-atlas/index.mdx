The umbrella project formerly known as `fretboard-explorer`. Multi-instrument
music theory tools, with the Barry Harris 6th-diminished scale tool as the
current centerpiece. The theory engine ([[harmonia]]) lives as its own Rust
crate, dependency-free.

## Current focus

Drop-2 voicing overlay on the 6th-diminished tool. The idea is to *show* the
voicings as fretboard shapes rather than explain them in prose.

## Why this matters

Most music theory tools *explain*. I want to build ones that *reveal* — so
the structure shows up in the artifact itself and you can feel it under your
hands before you have language for it.
